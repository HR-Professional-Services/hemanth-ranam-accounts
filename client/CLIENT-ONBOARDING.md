## 2. Invoicing SOP
1. Create new client profile in **Client Directory**.
2. Click **+ New Invoice**, add line items and tax rate.
3. Click **View PDF** to generate printable/downloadable invoice.
4. Record incoming client bank payments under `/api/invoices/{id}/payments`.
